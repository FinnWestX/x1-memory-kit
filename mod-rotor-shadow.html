FWX Helicopter Rotor Shadow.

for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024

The shadow that disappears exactly when you need it. On these helicopters the simulator only shows the rotor shadow while the rotor is still turning slowly - from medium rpm upwards it is gone completely, which is the whole of your flight. This add-on gives your machine its turning shadow back, at full rotor speed as well.

From the makers of the FWX EC135 Sound Mod, reviewed by SimFlight, Threshold and MSFS Addons.

What it does

On seven helicopters in the simulator the rotor shadow is only there at rest and while the rotor spools up. As the rpm rises it vanishes completely - for exactly the time you spend flying. This add-on closes that gap: a turning rotor disc on the ground, on the fuselage and on everything you fly over, at full rotor speed too.

It leans with the aircraft and stays with you - on every low pass, every approach and every landing. Main rotor and tail rotor are handled separately, so each casts its own shadow instead of both ending up as one dark blob.

Every supported type was measured individually, so the shadow belongs to the machine it falls from.

What you notice in the cockpit

  • The ground tells you where you are. On a low pass the turning disc runs across the surface below you - the thing your eye looks for and could not find at flying rpm.
  • It leans when you lean. Bank the aircraft and the shadow follows the disc, not a flat circle painted on the ground.
  • It does not stop at the ground. Fuselage, apron, rooftops, whatever is underneath you - the shadow falls on it.
  • Tail rotor included. The small disc at the back gets its own shadow, which is exactly what you see in a real photograph.
  • Landings read better. Height and drift over a confined area are easier to judge with a shadow moving in front of you.
  • Set once, kept per helicopter. What you set on one type is there again the next time you fly it.

Which helicopters

Seven types, each one measured individually:

  • Airbus H125
  • Airbus H135 (EC135)
  • Airbus H225
  • Guimbal Cabri G2
  • Robinson R66
  • Sikorsky S-64 Skycrane
  • Boeing CH-47 Chinook - both rotors
New helicopters are included
If the simulator gets another helicopter without a rotor shadow, it will be added free of charge with an update.

Using the panel

You set everything inside the simulator. The FWX Control Center is a panel in the simulator itself: you open it from the toolbar in mid-flight, set what you want, and close it again. There is no window outside the simulator you have to operate or keep running - nothing to alt-tab to just to change a setting.

The panel has one page per FWX add-on - Flight Realism, Rotor Shadow, Haptic, Sound Pack - plus a News page with our current notes. The master switch sits at the top, everything belonging to that page below it. Every change takes effect at once, without a restart.

The Rotor Shadow page is deliberately short - there is nothing to tune here:

  • Master switch. Shadow on or off, in mid-flight, without a restart.
  • The detected helicopter is shown next to it, so you can see at a glance whether the machine you are sitting in is one of the supported types.
  • The profile for this machine. The panel shows whether a saved setting of its own applies to this helicopter or the factory default.
  • Saving happens by itself. There is no save button to think about: what you set is there again the next time you fly that machine.
  • A sign of life. One line tells you at any time whether the add-on is working.

The strength of the shadow is deliberately fixed and always runs at full - the real rotor speed - whatever machine you are running the simulator on. There is no slider here to get wrong.

No extra program to operate
There is no Windows application to set anything in. The only switch there is sits in the panel inside the simulator.

What it does not do

  • It works on the seven types above only. Every other helicopter stays untouched - nothing changes there.
  • It changes nothing about the flight behaviour and nothing about the sound.
  • It does not change the aircraft files and does not overwrite any originals. It runs next to your own liveries and your other add-ons.

Compatibility

  • Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024. PC, Steam or Microsoft Store. MSFS 2020 is not supported.
  • Windows 11. Not available on Xbox, which cannot install third-party add-ons.
  • One of the seven supported helicopters. They are listed above.
  • No extra hardware. Whatever you already fly with is enough.
