NEW · 2.2.0 · SEPTEMBER 2026 Version 2.2.0 – major update for Heli Flight Dynamics

A completely renewed core system for Flight Dynamics with new factory profiles tuned in flight, a redesigned FWX Control Center with tutorial and manuals in English and German, and a revised installer. Important: profiles saved with earlier versions can no longer be used and are removed during the update – details below.

Major update for Heli Flight Dynamics · Version 2.2.0

v2.2.0

All products Current · NEW
September 2026

Important – profiles from earlier versions

  • This update brings a completely renewed core system for Flight Dynamics. Because of this, profiles saved with earlier versions can no longer be used and have been removed during the update. All helicopters start with our new, carefully tuned FWX factory profiles. Adjust anything you like and press Save – your own profile is created automatically, and our factory profile always stays untouched as a safe fallback. Future updates will keep your profiles.

Heli Flight Dynamics

  • New factory profiles, tuned in flight, for Cabri, R66, EC135, H125, H225, Bell 407, UH-1H, CH-47D and S-64F. Other helicopters start from a general base profile.
  • Helicopters without an FWX factory profile start with a neutral (tandem) rotor direction – set your helicopter’s rotor direction in the panel.
  • All sliders now use one clear 0–100 scale; the + / – buttons move in single steps.
  • Hover and forward flight (now called “Flight in motion”) can be set independently of each other.
  • Roll and yaw in the hover now act continuously.
  • New slider “Speed gain”: the faster you fly, the stronger the airflow effects.
  • New section “Effect strength & response” with “Overall Strength of Effect” (per helicopter), “Output smoothing” and “Effect build-up speed”.
  • The effect strength now adapts automatically to the size and weight distribution of each helicopter.
  • Tandem-rotor helicopters such as the Chinook are supported (rotor direction “Tandem”).
  • Fixed: when changing helicopters, a manually selected main rotor direction could be reset. The rotor direction now always comes from your profile.
  • Ground effect is now a setting inside the Hover section.
  • Fixed: in certain situations a jolt could occur in the transition between hover and forward flight.
  • Streamlined controls: clearer sliders. “Liveliness” is now called “Mechanical Dynamics”.
  • Profiles: Save, Save As, Load and Factory settings. Create as many own profiles as you like; your own profile is created with the first Save. Factory profiles can never be overwritten or deleted. Saving never overwrites a profile of another helicopter. Save As applies to all variants of the same helicopter.
  • No more automatic saving: changes are kept when you press Save. “(unsaved)” is shown only when a value really differs from your saved state.
  • At start, when changing helicopters, on every new flight and when coming back from an airplane, your last saved state is loaded – including the master switch.
  • Red marks on each slider show your saved value, and every slider has its own reset button back to it. “Factory settings” returns the whole helicopter to its factory profile.
  • Fixed: in certain situations one press of the master switch button could switch more than once. Now one press switches exactly once.
  • Airplanes: Flight Dynamics works with helicopters only. With an airplane loaded, all FWX tabs are greyed out with a clear notice, and the master switch button has no effect.

Haptic

  • The Haptic tab now shows the number of rotor blades of the current helicopter.
  • The messages of the Haptic program have been revised and unified.
  • The Haptic tab has a clearer layout.

Rotor Shadow

  • The R66 shadow display has been aligned with the other helicopters.
  • The messages of the Rotor Shadow program have been revised and unified.
  • Fixed: with the Cabri, the Rotor Shadow tab could show “not detected”. The tab now also shows the short helicopter name.

Sound

  • Sound tab: with any aircraft other than the EC135 loaded, the status now reads “NOT EC135”.
  • Clearer hint in the Sound tab on choosing your headphones or speakers.

FWX Control Center panel

  • New name “FWX Control Center”; the tab is now called “Flight Dynamics”.
  • New help tab “?” with a step-by-step tutorial with pictures, and the full manual – including the new chapter “How profiles work”.
  • A question mark on every section and slider explains it (opens when you point at it).
  • Four text sizes, including a large size for VR; the choice is stored separately for VR and 2D.
  • Choice of background: black, white or transparent.
  • Collapsible sections with one “Collapse / Expand all sections” button; tidier layout and larger buttons.
  • Tidier status displays (“Connected” / “Not running”).
  • Tip added: fly with live weather – the effects are most noticeable there.

Installer

  • Fixed: when adding another FWX product, the automatic start of FWX products already installed could be switched off.
  • The master switch button can be freely assigned in the panel.
  • The installer checks that the simulator is closed before it starts.
  • Fixed: if in individual cases a file could not be written, the installer still reported success. Now “Installation INCOMPLETE” appears with the affected files.
  • Uninstalling is now also possible through the Windows settings.
  • Packages with Flight Dynamics also include the manual as PDF in English and German.
  • Profiles from earlier versions are removed during the update (see item 1).
