What it does

Nothing here is random, scripted or invented. Every response is worked out live, moment by moment, from what your helicopter is doing at that very instant and from your own control inputs.

The result is that the aircraft behaves the way its own aerodynamics say it should: it breathes, drifts and settles instead of sitting in the air as if it were on rails. A hover is something you hold, not something the simulator holds for you. Speed, weight and attitude all change the feel, because they are the numbers the effect is built from.

How strongly all of this comes through stays your choice, separately for each helicopter.

What you notice in the cockpit

  • A hover you have to fly. The aircraft moves under you and asks for small corrections instead of parking itself.
  • Speed changes the character. Slow work, cruise and fast runs each feel different, because the underlying airflow is different.
  • Weight and attitude are felt, not announced. A heavy machine answers differently from a light one.
  • It stays flyable. The aircraft is lively, never twitchy - stable at all times, with no tendency to run away from you.
  • Your inputs stay in charge. The effect works with the controls in your hands, it never fights them.
  • Every helicopter keeps its own feel. What you set on one aircraft does not follow you to the next.

Profiles and controls

The add-on is built around one idea: set a helicopter up once, then never think about it again.

  1. One profile per helicopter type. Every type carries its own settings. Switch aircraft and the right profile comes with it - there is no single global setting that has to be a compromise between a light single and a heavy twin.
  2. Nine types are ready from the start. Nine helicopter types ship with a finished profile - EC135, H125, H225, Bell 407, Cabri G2, R66, CH-47D Chinook, S-64F Skycrane and UH-1H Huey - so the first flight is a flight and not a setup session. Every other helicopter starts from a neutral default you can shape yourself.
  3. Set to full strength, not to a guess. There is no master slider you have to balance before take-off. The effect runs at its intended strength, and the profile is there for the character of the aircraft, not for damage control.
  4. Save, name, load. Build your own version of a helicopter's feel, give it a name you recognise, and load it whenever you like. Save As keeps the original untouched next to it.
  5. Stable at all times. Built-in limits keep the aircraft from ever breaking away, whatever profile you load and however you fly it. The life stays in, the surprises stay out.
Your saved profiles are yours
A profile you saved stays exactly as you saved it. Nothing overwrites it behind your back, and nothing is changed by an update.

Using the panel

You set everything inside the simulator. The FWX Control Center is a panel in the simulator itself: you open it from the toolbar in mid-flight, set what you want, and close it again. There is no window outside the simulator you have to operate or keep running - nothing to alt-tab to just to change a setting.

The panel has one page per FWX add-on - Flight Realism, Rotor Shadow, Haptic, Sound Pack - plus a News page with our current notes. The master switch sits at the top, everything belonging to that page below it. Every change takes effect at once, without a restart.

The Flight Realism page holds everything this add-on is about:

  • Master switch. One click and the effect is on or off, in flight, straight away.
  • Two separate blocks: forward flight and hover. Both states are set on their own - what is right in the cruise is often too much in the hover. Each block keeps its own values and can be collapsed.
  • Inside each block: gusts (short pushes from the air) and below them the three axes pitch, yaw and roll - each with a slider for airflow and one for liveliness, and each one switchable on its own.
  • Block strength. One slider that makes everything in its block stronger or calmer at once. At 0 nothing in that block moves any more.
  • Ground effect. The cushion of air close to the ground - off from the factory, because it is the one slider that reaches into a control you hold in your own hand.
  • Tail rotor lever arm. Where the tail rotor sits relative to the centre of gravity. Zero means no effect.
  • Main rotor direction. Detected for you and shown on screen; your own choice is kept if you make one.
  • A question mark on every block. One click unfolds an explanation in plain English - no manual to keep open beside you.
  • Collapse what you do not need. The panel stays readable.

Profiles - on the same page

Profile handling sits at the top of the same page, not in a second program:

  • A list of your saved profiles, grouped into this helicopter, presets and other helicopters. A profile you built for one type can therefore be loaded in another.
  • Save, Save As, Load. Save As asks for a name and puts the new profile next to the old one without touching it.
  • Reset to defaults. That only becomes permanent once you save afterwards - until then you can still change your mind.
  • Delete with a confirmation. The profile currently in use cannot be deleted.
  • Always visible: which profile is loaded, whether you have changed anything since, and whether the add-on is running.
No extra program to operate
There is no Windows application to type values into and no file to edit by hand. Everything this add-on can do is set in the panel inside the simulator - where you fly.

What it does not do

  • It does not replace a flight model.
  • It works on helicopters only, not on airplanes.
  • It changes nothing about the sound and nothing about the visuals.

Compatibility

  • Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024. PC, Steam or Microsoft Store. MSFS 2020 is not supported.
  • Windows 11. Not available on Xbox, which cannot install third-party add-ons.
  • A helicopter, any one. It works on every helicopter in the simulator. Nine types arrive with a ready-made profile; every other helicopter starts from a neutral default that you shape and save under its own name.
  • No extra hardware. Whatever controls you already fly with are enough.
